📌 1. The "j, q, x, y + i / ü" Rule Notebook Page 2 Top
[ j , q , x , y ] + [ i / ü ]
✍️ Write Branch (Dots Dropped on ü!):
write ──► ju , qu , xu , yu
Why? Because j, q, x, y NEVER combine with normal "u" ("oo"), so the two dots on ü are redundant in writing!
🗣️ Pronounce Branch (Keep Rounded Whistle Lips!):
pronounce ──► juy (jü) , quy (qü) , xuy (xü) , yuy (yü)
🎯 2. "Putting Tones" — Vowel Priority Hierarchy Where Does the Tone Mark Go?
◄─────────────────────────── Highest Priority on Left
a  ◄──  o  ◄──  e  ◄──  i , u , ü
When a syllable has multiple vowels, place the tone mark on the highest-priority vowel!
Rule A (a > o > e): If a is present, it ALWAYS gets the tone (, ). No a? Look for o or e (, ).
Rule B (Exception for "iu" & "ui"): When i and u appear together (iu = iou, ui = uei), the tone mark ALWAYS goes on the SECOND (last) vowel: , .
Rule C (Dot on "i" vs "ü"): When i gets a tone mark, its single dot disappears (i ──► ǐ in qǐng 请). When ü keeps its dots (like nǚ 女 = female), the tone sits above the two dots!

[DIAGRAM 5.4] ──► Follow Me to Pinyin (跟我学拼音) — Tone Sandhi of "一 (yī)" ("一"的变调)
1️⃣ When "一" Stays 1st Tone (yī ─►)
When counting alone, in ordinal numbers (第一 dì yī), or at the end of a word, 一 keeps its original 1st tone (yī):
Core Slide Formula
yī 一 + 一声 / 二声 / 三声 ──► yī 变四声 yì (↘)
yī 一 + 四声 (4th) ──► yī 变二声 yí (↗)
2️⃣ Tone Sandhi Transformation Tree: yī (一) Before Measure Words
Rule A: Before 1st (─), 2nd (╱), or 3rd (∨) Tone ──► Flips to 4th Tone yì (↘)
Rule B: Before 4th Tone (╲) ──► Flips to 2nd Tone yí (↗)
Just like bù ──► bú before a 4th tone, yī rises to 2nd tone yí before any 4th-tone syllable:

⚙️ Grammar: 连动句 (1) Serial Verb Sentences & “怎么”
(1) Purpose (目的): S + V1 (go) + Place + V2 (purpose)
V2 ("buy things") is the purpose of V1 ("go to supermarket").
(2) Manner (方式): S + V1 (transport) + V2 (go + place)
Transport/manner ALWAYS comes BEFORE destination!
